Mr Medaka's Pond: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Mr Medaka's Pond is a single-player game about keeping medaka. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own.

What the game stores

Your ponds, fish, coins, settings and the name you give yourself as a breeder are saved on your phone only. Postcards you choose to save go into your phone's Pictures folder. If you have Android's own backup turned on, Android may include this game's saves in your device backup to your Google account. That is handled by Google under your Android backup settings, not by us. We never see it.

Trading with a friend

Trading happens directly between two phones held near each other, using Google Play services' Nearby Connections (Bluetooth and Wi-Fi). It only runs while the Trade screen is open.

  • To find a nearby phone, Android asks for the Nearby devices permission (on older Android versions this is shown as Location, because Bluetooth scanning falls under it). The game does not read, store or send your location.
  • What passes between the two phones is only what a trade needs: your breeder name, your in-game character's look, a random in-game ID, which colours and traits your collection book already holds (so offers can be marked as new), the fish you offer (their names, varieties, looks, family records and an in-game price) and any thoughts you share. Nothing goes to any server.
  • On your phone, the game keeps the fish and thoughts the other player gave you, a line in your pond journal, and a short list of friends you have traded with (their breeder name, character look, in-game ID, the date, and what was swapped), so you can see them on the Trade screen. Nothing else is kept about the other player.
  • Nearby Connections is part of Google Play services, which may process connection metadata under Google's privacy policy.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
