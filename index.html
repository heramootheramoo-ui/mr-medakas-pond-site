Mr Medaka's Pond

A slow, calm game about keeping and breeding medaka in a little backyard pond.

Get it on Google Play

What it's like

  • Real water chemistry: bacteria, nitrate, green water and the seasons.
  • Real breeding, with genetics, recessive genes and line breeding.
  • Fish with natures of their own.
  • Your own yard of buckets, tubs and ponds.
  • Trade fish face to face with a friend, phone to phone.
  • Gentle sayings from Zen, the Tao Te Ching and Zhuangzi.

No ads. No accounts. No streaks. No nagging notifications.
